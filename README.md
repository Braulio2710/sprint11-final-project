# 🏢 Andes Capital Real Estate – Commercial Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project develops an interactive **Power BI dashboard** for Andes Capital Real Estate, a real estate company that manages property sales across different customer segments, property types, cities, and sales channels.

The objective is to transform transactional sales data into a clear analytical view of **revenue, sales performance, customer behavior, temporal trends, and purchase recurrence**.

The project focuses not only on presenting metrics, but also on identifying patterns and insights that can support strategic commercial decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The dashboard was designed to answer key business questions:

- What is the total revenue generated by property sales?
- Which property type generates the most revenue?
- Which customer segments generate the most sales and revenue?
- Which sales channel contributes the most revenue?
- How do sales evolve over time?
- Is the business growing year over year?
- Do customers return to purchase after their first transaction?
- Which customer cohorts generate the highest revenue and sales over time?

---

## 📂 Data Model

The project uses a **star schema** composed of a central fact table and dimension tables.

### Fact table

**`hecho_ventas_propiedades`**

Contains one record for each property sale.

Key fields include:

- `id_venta`
- `fecha_venta`
- `id_cliente`
- `id_propiedad`
- `precio_venta`
- `tipo_propiedad`
- `canal_venta`
- `porcentaje_comision`
- `monto_comision`

### Customer dimension

**`dim_clientes`**

Contains customer information and segmentation:

- `id_cliente`
- `segmento_comprador`
- `pais`
- `ciudad`

### Property dimension

**`dim_propiedades`**

Contains property characteristics:

- `id_propiedad`
- `tipo_propiedad`
- `ciudad`
- `barrio`
- `habitaciones`
- `tamano_m2`
- `precio_publicado`
- `categoria_propiedad`

### Date dimension

**`dim_fecha`**

A calendar table created specifically for temporal analysis.

It includes fields such as:

- `Date`
- `Año`
- `Mes`
- `Mes Numero`
- `Año-Mes`

The date dimension enables time-intelligence calculations such as YoY and YTD.

## 📐 Analytical Measures

The Power BI model includes several DAX measures organized around the main business questions.

### Base measures

- **Total Revenue**
- **Number of Sales**
- **Average Ticket**
- **Total Commission**

### Revenue participation

Additional measures calculate the percentage contribution of:

- Property type
- Sales channel
- Customer segment

These measures are used as complementary information in dashboard tooltips.

### Time intelligence

The model also includes time-based calculations such as:

- Year-to-Date (YTD)
- Previous-year revenue
- Year-over-Year (YoY) growth

### Cohort analysis

Calculated columns were created to identify:

- First purchase date by customer
- Cohort month
- Sale month

These fields allow the construction of a cohort matrix to analyze customer recurrence over time.

## 🖥️ Dashboard Structure

The dashboard contains three analytical views.

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of business performance.

Key elements include:

- Total Revenue
- Number of Sales
- Average Ticket
- Total Commission
- Revenue trends over time
- Revenue by city
- Year-over-Year growth

The objective is to allow an executive user to understand the overall state of the business quickly.

---

### 2. Commercial Analysis

Provides a deeper analysis of the main commercial drivers.

It includes:

- Revenue by property type
- Revenue by sales channel
- Revenue by customer segment
- Performance table with conditional formatting
- Revenue participation through interactive tooltips

This view helps identify where revenue is being generated and how different commercial dimensions contribute to overall performance.

---

### 3. Customer Cohort Analysis

Analyzes customer recurrence after the first purchase.

The cohort matrix uses:

- **Rows:** Cohort month
- **Columns:** Sale month
- **Values:** Sales or revenue

This makes it possible to identify customers who return to purchase and compare the behavior of different acquisition cohorts over time.

## 📌 Key Findings

### Revenue and overall performance

The business generated approximately **$6.01 billion** in total revenue from **8,500 sales**, with an average ticket of approximately **$707,353** and total commissions of approximately **$200.6 million**.

Revenue increased from approximately **$2.85 billion in 2023 to $3.16 billion in 2024**, representing **11.14% year-over-year growth**.

### Property performance

The three property types show different performance profiles:

| Property Type | Revenue | Sales | Average Ticket |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| House | $2.24B | 2,324 | $964K |
| Commercial | $1.92B | 1,071 | $1.80M |
| Apartment | $1.85B | 5,105 | $362K |

One of the most relevant findings is that **the property type with the highest sales volume is not the one with the highest revenue or average ticket**.

- Apartments generate the highest number of sales.
- Houses generate the highest total revenue.
- Commercial properties generate the highest average ticket.

This demonstrates the importance of analyzing multiple KPIs instead of relying exclusively on sales volume.

### Geographic performance

**Bogotá** generated approximately **$3.24 billion**, compared with approximately **$2.77 billion** generated by Mexico City.

### Sales channels

The **Broker** channel generated approximately **$4.38 billion**, significantly exceeding the **Direct** channel at approximately **$1.63 billion**.

### Customer segments

The **First-time Buyer** segment generated the highest revenue, with approximately **$3.78 billion**, followed by Investors and High Net Worth customers.

### Customer cohorts

The March and April 2023 cohorts showed particularly strong performance.

- March 2023 generated approximately **$295.6 million** and 373 sales.
- April 2023 generated approximately **$244.9 million** and 365 sales.

Both cohorts also show subsequent purchases over time, providing evidence of recurring customer activity.

---

## 💡 Business Insights

Several insights emerge from combining revenue, sales volume, average ticket, and cohort behavior.

### Volume does not necessarily equal value

Apartments account for the largest number of transactions, but their lower average ticket means they do not generate the highest revenue.

Commercial properties have substantially fewer transactions but the highest average ticket.

This suggests that commercial strategy should distinguish between **volume-driven and value-driven products**.

### Revenue concentration

Broker-generated sales represent the largest contribution to revenue. This makes the channel strategically important, but also highlights the importance of monitoring its performance and dependence.

### Customer acquisition and retention

First-time buyers represent the largest revenue-generating customer segment.

The cohort analysis also shows that some acquisition cohorts generate meaningful subsequent purchases, suggesting opportunities to strengthen post-purchase engagement and customer retention.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- Prioritize the commercialization of **House properties**, given their leading contribution to total revenue.
- Maintain and optimize the **Broker channel**, given its significant contribution to overall revenue.
- Develop differentiated strategies by property type, balancing the high volume of Apartments with the high-value transactions generated by Commercial properties.
- Strengthen retention strategies after the first purchase, particularly for the First-time Buyer segment.
- Investigate the characteristics of the strongest cohorts, particularly March and April 2023, to identify practices that could be replicated in future acquisition cohorts.
- Monitor YoY revenue growth together with sales volume, average ticket, and commissions rather than relying on a single KPI.
- Analyze opportunities for growth in Mexico City and the Direct sales channel.
